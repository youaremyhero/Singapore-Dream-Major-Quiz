## Cohort distinction
The FASS Bulletin's department list uses 'Chinese Language' and 'Chinese Studies'; NUS Chinese Studies department specifically states:
- Cohort 2026 onwards: **Chinese Languages and Cultures** and **Chinese Studies (Bilingual)** each have distinct direct-Honours routes.
- Cohort 2025 or earlier: former **Chinese Language** and **Chinese Studies** arrangements continue under their respective cohort rules.

Per project policy, do not present old cohort names as additional current programmes. Keep them for history/alias reconciliation only.
