# Programme catalogue presentation policy

Decision confirmed by project owner, 15 October 2026.

## Student-facing recommendations
- **Academic major first** where available, followed by the **actual official application pathway**. Example: Finance — NUS; Apply via Business Administration.
- Do not invent a standalone admission choice for a major that is reached through a broader admission pathway.
- Record types must remain distinct: undergraduate degree, first major, admission choice, second major, minor, double degree, special track.
- Double degrees and special/limited-entry tracks appear **only in Explore More**, with official eligibility and cohort caveats where known. They do not inflate top-five primary matches.
- Second majors are discoverable separately and should not be mistaken for standalone admission programmes.
- Retain NUS minors in the data and optional exploration catalogue; **exclude minors for all other AUs**.

## Current-versus-historical status
- **Renamed and no-longer-offered programmes must not appear as current quiz options**, recommendations, or default discovery results.
- Preserve historical programme names and discontinued offerings only as archive/aliases for reconciliation and search, explicitly not offered as an additional active programme.
- Never infer discontinuation from a missing link or an unverified page. Only hide as retired/renamed when supported by the university's own information.
- For an official rename, show the current title and retain the former title as an alias in the underlying catalogue.
- If the current status cannot be established, flag it for review; do not fabricate a current status.

## NUS Computer Engineering
- Display school/department association as **SoC / CDE** (joint programme), not two separate Computer Engineering degrees.
- Existing NUS-0148 and NUS-0157 are reconciliation candidates representing the same subject; resolve into one canonical display entry while retaining old IDs as linked historical records.
- The provided image shows two departments' contact details; these are not themselves proof of current degree title, admissions rules, or a programme-specific URL.

## Source and link policy
Only official AU websites may support degree titles, availability, cohort restrictions, admission routes and URLs. Index-level links must be identified as such. Do not invent links.
The provisional catalogue remains usable internally while each school's official audit proceeds. Student display must filter confirmed retired names and historical aliases.
