# NUS catalogue official audit — Geospatial Intelligence and professional schools

Date: 2026-10-15. Official NUS sources only.

## New programme confirmed and corrected in working catalogue
- **Geospatial Intelligence** is a four-year BSc (Honours) Cross-Disciplinary Programme (GI-XDP) jointly offered by FASS Geography and NUS School of Computing.
- NUS CHS programme page: https://chs.nus.edu.sg/programmes/gi/
- NUS AY2026 application form lists **Geospatial Intelligence** as its own choice under CHS: https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/docs/default-source/default-document-library/cat-d-sample-application-form.pdf?sfvrsn=91ae82c8_5
- This programme already existed as **NUS-0145** in the working catalogue, but only linked to the CHS index and had a generic label. **Updated that record in place** to direct programme URL, FASS / SoC joint offering and confirmed application choice, rather than duplicating.
- New independent audit file: `data/official_audit/NUS_Geospatial_Intelligence_AY2026_27.csv`.

## Professional degrees — primary batch
Sources are the NUS official AY2026/27 Bulletin and school-controlled programme pages; see the exact per-row links in `data/official_audit/NUS_Professional_Schools_AY2026_27.csv`.

1. Medicine — five-year **MBBS** (Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine).
2. Nursing — **BSc (Nursing) / BSc (Nursing)(Honours)** (Alice Lee Centre): three-year BSc plus possible honours progression. Do not show a duplicate standalone honours degree to incoming students.
3. Dentistry — four-year **Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS)**; honours distinction does not constitute a new entry choice.
4. Law — four-year **LLB (Honours)**; admission has additional assessment for shortlisted candidates.
5. Music — four-year **Bachelor of Music (Honours)** at Yong Siew Toh; the Bulletin describes 20 specific majors, but this batch only confirms the degree and general major structure, not a 20-item individually verified list.

## Special offerings deliberately excluded from default primary recommendations
- Part-time **BSc (Nursing Practice)** for nursing diploma holders/working nurses; this is not the same full-time pre-university degree path.
- Music majors and instrumental specialisations are not distinct bachelor-degree awards; map them beneath BMus when their current availability is individually established.
- Additional specialised programme/track types follow the existing **Explore More** policy.
