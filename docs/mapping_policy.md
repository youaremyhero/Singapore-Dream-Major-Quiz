# Programme mapping and recommendation policy

A student selects interests, **not an ability level**. Profile statements describe stated preferences, not measured aptitudes.

1. Score academic families only from the 20 core questions. A question may contribute at most one independent evidence source for the same signal regardless of two selected options. Neutral, unsure and skipped responses contribute no negative evidence.
2. Dive Deeper adds direct optional evidence; it never changes preserved core family scores. Student may browse any family.
3. Current engine's ≥2 distinct questions and a ≥2-question peer lead are provisional conservative test gates, not validated aptitude measures or final numerical weights.
4. Never infer a programme's direct-entry status from its name. Distinguish programme, parent admission pathway, second major, minor and double degree.
5. Display verified academic family when discipline evidence is weak; display specific programme only from human-reviewed `approved_programme_mappings.csv`. An empty ledger means **no official programme shortlist**, even if machine suggestions exist.
6. Source `Why This Fits` from selected response IDs, quote or paraphrase chosen options, and explain relevant academic activities. Avoid percentages and certainty claims.
7. Check official programme pages, degree title, admissions route, and availability before approving mappings. Track review dates.
8. Re-run regression tests after annual AU catalogue refresh. New programmes must not change interest-score calculations.
